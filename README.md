# ⛪ Deepening Growth in Committed Churches through Enhanced Congregational Practical Theological Interpretation

**Organization:** Open Hearts Training Center (OHTC) - Cap-Haitian, Haiti

> Equipping 300 congregational leaders across northern and central Haiti with practical theological tools to strengthen church ministry and spiritual growth.


## 📋 Project Description

This training project serves **300 congregational leaders** across **Cap-Haitian**, the **Northern Department**, and **Hinche** in the Central Department of Haiti. Through hands-on workshops and structured training sessions, leaders are equipped to apply Practical Theology in their ministry contexts.

Participants in this program will:
- Understand and apply Practical Theological interpretation in church ministry
- Analyze real-life ministry situations in light of Scripture
- Design and implement outreach initiatives
- Write short theological reflections on ministry situations
- Use evidence-based tools for practical theological reflection
- Receive a **certificate of completion**

## 🎯 Objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | Train **12 trainers** in Practical Theological Interpretation by end of Month 1 |
| 2 | Establish a **learning and exchange forum** engaging 300 congregational leaders by end of Month 2 |
| 3 | Distribute **600 training manuals** and resources to participating leaders |
| 4 | Improve church **ministry effectiveness** by at least **25%** |
| 5 | Strengthen understanding of Practical Theological Interpretation among leaders |
